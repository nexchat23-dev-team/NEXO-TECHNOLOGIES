Security Best Practices

Strong Authentication

Multi-factor authentication, hardware keys, passwordless auth. Never rely on passwords alone for critical systems.

Encryption Everywhere

AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, end-to-end for communications. Assume all channels are compromised.

Principle of Least Privilege

Grant only the minimum permissions required. Regularly audit and revoke unnecessary access rights.

Defense in Depth

Layered security controls. Network segmentation, host-based firewalls, application security, monitoring.

Patch Management

Keep all systems updated. Subscribe to CVE feeds, use automated patching where safe, prioritize critical vulnerabilities.

Continuous Monitoring

SIEM integration, anomaly detection, SOC operations. You can't defend what you can't see. Log everything.
